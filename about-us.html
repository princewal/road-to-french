Our approach

Practical, structured, and built around evidence.

We begin with your actual level rather than a generic starting point. From there, every session connects to the exam, profile, or communication goal you are working toward.

For federal candidates, that means understanding the SLE rubric, the profile your role requires, and the format of the oral interview. For PR candidates, it means building all four skills toward the official TEF Canada or TCF Canada result.
