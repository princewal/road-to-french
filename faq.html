Do you prepare for the SLE as well as the TCF/TEF?

Yes. We run Federal SLE routes, including a dedicated Oral Proficiency Track for candidates targeting Level C oral, as well as TEF Canada and TCF Canada preparation.

What is NCLC 7?

NCLC 7 is a French-language benchmark used for Canadian immigration. The PR program prepares you for the four skills assessed through TEF Canada or TCF Canada.

What do BBB, CBC, and CCC mean?

They are SLE profiles in the order Reading, Writing, Oral. Each letter indicates Level A, B, or C in that skill.

How large are the group programs?

Groups are kept to 7–9 learners and matched by level.

How does placement work?

Start with a level test so we can identify your current ability, goal, and the appropriate route.

How long is a module?

The standard module is 75 hours. Your total timeline depends on your starting level and target.

Do you administer the official tests?

No. TEF/TCF and SLE tests are administered by their official organizations. We prepare you for the format and skills they assess.

Can I retake an SLE test right away?

A 30-day wait applies before retesting. Your coach can help you use that time to target the skills that need work.

Do you offer private coaching?

Yes. Private sessions are available for PR, SLE, interviews, or a specific weakness. See the Private Coaching page for options.
