Know your profile

Every SLE profile is a three-letter code.

The letters always appear in this order: Reading comprehension, Written expression, Oral proficiency. Each letter is a level: A, B, or C.

BBB means Level B in all three skills. CBC means Level C in reading and oral, with Level B in writing. CCC means Level C across the profile.

Your required profile depends on the position and staffing process. Check the job poster or confirm the exact requirement with your HR advisor or staffing contact before choosing a route.
